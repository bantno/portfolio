Teams write autonomy software that pilots racing drones through professional courses with **zero human intervention**, competing for a share of a **$500,000** prize pool and engineering roles at Anduril.

## The Round-2 Problem

Round 2 strips away every absolute reference. What the simulator will still tell you, and what it won't:

- **Available:** a raw IMU (body-frame specific force and body rates, ~80 Hz), a 640×360 onboard camera at ~30 Hz, collision events, and a race-status gate counter.
- **Disabled:** GPS, attitude, position, odometry — those fields come back null — **and the gate coordinates themselves.**
- **Control surface:** body rates plus collective throttle. The actuator and position/velocity setpoint paths are dead.

So the stack has to recover attitude, velocity, and position from IMU and vision alone, then fly a geometric controller through gate openings **1.5 m square**. Three subsystems carry that: a gate detector, a 15-state error-state Kalman filter, and an SE(3) tracking controller.

## Gate Detection

The only source of gate localization — each camera frame becomes range, bearing, and where resolvable a full 6-DOF gate pose, each tagged with explicit trust flags telling the estimator which parts to believe.

- **Segmentation is brightness-invariant.** Gate markers pulse, so absolute HSV floors produce an unstable blob frame to frame. The shipped mask keys on **red dominance** — `R − max(G,B) ≥ 30` and `R ≥ 45` — closed with a 5×5 morphological kernel to bridge the blue targeting beam that otherwise severs the ring into a C-arc.
- **Non-gate red is rejected structurally, not by hue.** A real gate is a hollow, near-square ring; the ceiling arrows and the "AI-GP" banner are solid. A blob is dropped only when it is both solid *and* wide.
- **Pose recovery cascades by range.** A fully-visible ring solves through `SOLVEPNP_IPPE_SQUARE` against the 2.72 m outer border for full 6-DOF pose. As the gate clips the frame, the pipeline degrades deliberately — to the 1.5 m inner opening, then to a geometric construction from visible free edges, then to a bearing-only centroid — with each regime declaring its own trust level.
- **Range is only trusted when it's earned.** Measured blob edges floor at **~16.3 px** (glow halo, minimum line width, JPEG bleed), so beyond ~50 m every gate back-projects to the same ~44–53 m. Trust is therefore tested on *measured pixel size*, never the derived range, under one continuous law: `σ_range/range = σ_px/(edge_px − floor)` — **9% at 23 m, 83% at 47 m**, diverging at the floor.
- **Bearing has no such floor**, so a range-floored far gate still contributes an accurate bearing. That asymmetry is what lets the near gate anchor the along-track axis while far gates resect heading — and the handover happens automatically as gates approach.
- **Classical beat learned.** Zero-shot ML detectors (YOLO-World) produced no detections against the simulator's stylized aesthetic; classical thresholding is the shipped backend.
- **Bounded compute.** Blobs are processed largest-first under a 25 ms wall-clock budget, so a many-gate frame can't starve the receive and control threads. Truncation only ever drops the far tail.

## State Estimation

A 15-state error-state Kalman filter in the Solà (2017) formulation — nominal state `[p, v, q, a_b, ω_b]`, error state `[δp, δv, δθ, δa_b, δω_b]` with a body-frame attitude error.

- **The accelerometer is an input, not a measurement.** There is deliberately no thrust model, no hand-scheduled trust modulation, and no hand-gained residual feedback anywhere in the filter. The only state that can absorb a specific-force error is the accel bias, and it moves solely through a covariance-weighted Kalman gain bounded by its own process noise.
- **Exact Jacobians.** The covariance transition matrix is the true first-order Jacobian of the propagator — including the right-Jacobian factor the textbook form omits — proven against a numerical Jacobian in the test suite.
- **Four measurement types**, each with a closed-form Jacobian and χ²-gated innovation: body-frame landmark position against a frozen 17-gate map, gate-plane normal as the attitude anchor, a **rotor-drag** pseudo-measurement (Faessler 2018) that makes velocity observable with no vision at all, and a yaw pseudo-measurement. A pre-arm at-rest routine (ZUPT, gravity levelling, gyro bias) initializes the filter on the 17.8° launch ramp.
- **Reject, never force-fit.** Corrections use the Joseph form with Solà's post-injection reset. Gating is two-tier: beyond the 99.9% χ² quantile is a hard reject, while 95–99.9% inflates the measurement noise so a marginal fix is admitted only up to its validated magnitude. The state correction is never clamped — that would desynchronize the covariance from the state.
- **Camera latency is handled by rewinding, not approximating.** Vision fixes are stamped at frame capture and arrive after the filter has integrated past that epoch. A ~1.2 s ring of state snapshots lets the filter restore the nearest snapshot, apply the update *there*, and re-propagate the buffered IMU forward (the Lynen 2013 / `ethzasl_msf` pattern) — exact, at roughly four propagate steps.
- **Noise is measured, not tuned.** Each sighting's covariance is built from calibrated residual laws on the axes the detector actually errs along — including an attitude-rate-coupled centre wander treated as a *colored* process so a white-noise filter can't double-count it, and an independent misorientation witness: a bolted-down gate can't rotate 50° in 0.1 s, so the angle between the solved and mapped normal measures this frame's pose error directly.

## Control

A cascaded SE(3) geometric tracking controller in the Lee et al. (2010) lineage, ported from the Round-1 client.

- **Reference.** An arc-length-reparameterised cubic Hermite spline through the gate opening centres is the sole reference path.
- **Speed governor.** A progress variable marches along the spline at `min(flat cap, curvature cap √(g·tanθ/κ), descent cap)`, previewed and rate-limited.
- **Cascade.** Translational PD produces a world acceleration command, decomposed yaw-aware into roll/pitch setpoints under a tilt clamp; an attitude-P loop with feed-forward emits body rates.
- **Plant inversion.** Body rates are pushed through the exact inverse of the *measured* plant — a nonlinear rate gain, mount rotation, and sign chain mirrored precisely against the estimator's sensor mapping so sensing and command remain true inverses. Throttle is tilt-compensated through a calibrated affine thrust map.

## Verification

Flight accuracy is scored **only** against an independent ground-truth observer running as a separate read-only process — never against the estimator's own state.

- **Offline CI gate.** Two suites (`test_paper_stack.py`, `test_eskf15.py`) plus roughly 60 standalone tests, covering numeric-vs-analytic Jacobians, covariance symmetry and positive-definiteness, reset behaviour, reduction to known cases, and NIS/NEES consistency on synthetic data.
- **Per-run consistency reporting.** Channel-by-channel NIS statistics accumulate every run — mean ≈ degrees of freedom, ~5% beyond the 95% gate for a consistent filter.
- **Batch A/B harness.** Arm-tagged batches run from spec files with venue-health tripwires; findings accumulate as a version-controlled experiment corpus alongside the code.

## Results

- **First full-course completion** with all 17 gates registered and zero collisions: **22 July 2026**, pure vision.
- **Counted record: 19.78 s**, reached by successive tuning of the speed governor, corner authority, and per-gate aim, with the full config ladder recorded in the repo.
- **Estimator accuracy: ~0.8 m position RMS** against ground truth over 253 m of flight.
