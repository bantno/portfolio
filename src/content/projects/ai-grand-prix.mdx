## The Competition

The [AI Grand Prix](https://www.theaigrandprix.com/) is an autonomous drone racing competition conceived by Anduril founder Palmer Luckey, run with the Drone Champions League and Neros Technologies — a **$500,000** prize pool and engineering roles at Anduril. Teams qualify in simulation, then fly the same autonomy on a real drone, with the top ten advancing to the championship.

## Phase One — The Simulator Qualifier

The organisers disable GPS, attitude, position — **and the gate coordinates.** Everything the drone knows about the course, it has to see and solve for in flight.

I wrote the flight software end to end: gate perception from the camera, state estimation from the camera and IMU, and the guidance and control that fly the resulting path, all running in the loop in real time, together with the offline replay and test tooling used to tune it.

**Result: 7th of 3,000+ teams.** Best lap 17.56 s, all 17 gates, zero collisions, pure vision.

## Phase Two — The Physical Qualifier

The same stack has been brought up on real racing quadrotors, one subsystem at a time, with a safety pilot holding the arm and override switches throughout.

<Video src="/images/ai-grand-prix/onboard_gate_detection.mp4" caption="The gate detector running on onboard video from the qualifier venue." />

Where it stands, as of late September 2026:

- **Fully autonomous hover.** Three flights for three: take off, hold a commanded height for 30 s and land, with the pilot touching nothing but the arm and override switches.
- **Perception, estimation, guidance and control run in real time on the drone's own compute.**
- **Course laps.** Autonomous gate passages are confirmed on video, and the longest autonomous flight to date entered both laps of the course and logged 17 gates before the pilot took over. The full course has not yet been completed autonomously.
